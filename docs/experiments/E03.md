# E03: how deep does the mechanical map cut, and how many D1 items can a repository supply?

## Why this matters (30 seconds)

The primary outcome (P1) asks the owner about ordered pairs of components, and an item is
consumed by its first exposure (design 03 section 3.5). If a repository's mechanical map
gives no usable components, or its pair pool runs out before the confirmatory study has
the tasks it needs, the study is infeasible there however well the instrument works.
Design 6.6 puts the need at about 54 registered tasks for g = 0.54 and 200 for g = 0.28,
at 4 items consumed per task.

## Decision rule, written before the run (2026-10-19)

For each repository at its committed HEAD, read from a `git clone --local` under a
temporary `$PRAXIS_HOME` that is deleted at exit:

- A repository supplies the confirmatory primary on its own iff (a) `components.derive`
  gives 5 to 30 product components with no warning, and (b) its full-coverage D1 pool
  holds at least 216 items (54 tasks at 4 items per task).
- The repositories pooled supply it iff their full-coverage pools sum to 216. Whether
  they reach 800 items (200 tasks, g = 0.28) is reported, not ruled on.
- A D1 item is an ordered pair of product components that both have a Python file X1
  parsed. It is full-coverage when neither component has a blind spot or a parse failure.

Prediction written before the run: praxis alone fails (b).

## What we did

`experiments/E03/pool_depth.py` on praxis (`/home/user/praxis`), telltale
(`/home/user/telltale`) and systemap (`/home/user/0xfauzi/systemap`). It runs
`components.derive` on the clone, counts the product components the same cut gives at
every depth, runs `extract.static_imports` over the clone, and counts D1 pairs, edges,
partial components and D2 reach (a path but no direct edge). Two descriptive numbers sit
beside the counts. The first is the in-sample Brier of the D1 density, `b(1 - b)`. The
second is the in-sample Brier of a layer rule. That rule takes each strongly connected
group's longest path to a sink. A pair whose source is at the same height or higher gets
the forward edge rate snapped to the grid, and every other pair gets 0.1. A second run
with `--worktree` read praxis's working tree in place, uncommitted wave 2 files included.
That run is supplementary and is not part of the rule.

## What we measured

At HEAD (praxis e9b5e3a, telltale ec6a9e4, systemap 02a496b):

| | praxis | telltale | systemap |
|---|---|---|---|
| product root | `src/praxis` | `src/telltale` | `src/systemap` |
| product components at depth 1, 2, ... | 14 | 72, 92 | 163, 163 |
| map chosen | depth 1, 14 components | depth 0, 1 component, `depth_reduced_from_1_to_0` | depth 0, 1 component, `depth_reduced_from_1_to_0` |
| share of components that are one file | 1.0 | n/a | n/a |
| components with a blind spot | 1 (`store`) | 1 (`.`, 36 blind spots) | 1 (`.`, 7) |
| D1 pairs / edges | 182 / 14 (0.077) | 0 | 0 |
| full-coverage items / edges | 156 / 9 | 0 | 0 |
| D2 positive / negative | 11 / 157 | 0 | 0 |
| tasks the full pool lasts | 39 | 0 | 0 |
| in-sample Brier, density / layer rule | 0.0710 / 0.0715 | none | none |
| supplies the primary alone | no | no | no |

Pooled full-coverage items: 156, against the 216 the rule needs and the 800 for g = 0.28.
**Rule: FAIL**, alone and pooled. The prediction for praxis held.

Supplementary, praxis working tree on 2026-10-19 with B1 to B5's uncommitted modules: 18
product components, 306 pairs, 40 edges (0.131), partial components `hooks`, `recorder`
and `store` with 4 blind spots, 210 full-coverage items (23 edges), 52 tasks. That is 6
items short of 216. All 4 blind spots are `if TYPE_CHECKING` imports that resolve into
another praxis component. This was checked by running `extract._Sites` over the three
files, and none is a dynamic import. So 96 of the 306 pairs are partial only because of
type-only imports, which ruff's `TC` rules move under `TYPE_CHECKING` in this
repository.

## What it means

- The mechanical map does not work on flat packages. telltale has 72 top-level modules
  and systemap has 163. Depth 1 gives more than 30 components, so `derive` steps back to
  depth 0, which is one component. No structural item exists there until the owner
  freezes an owner map (design 2.4). `components.derive` returns that one-component map
  with a warning instead of raising `TooFewComponents`, so the refusal has to come from
  `praxis set components`, which refuses fewer than 5.
- praxis alone cannot supply even the g = 0.54 count. Pooled with the other two
  repositories under the mechanical map, the supply does not grow. Design 6.6 already
  said a conceptual-size effect is out of reach on imports alone. This run shows the
  large-effect count is also out of reach until owner maps exist for the flat
  repositories.
- At the current praxis tree the layer rule does not beat the density reference in
  sample (0.0715 against 0.0710). Design 4.1 measured 0.130 against 0.173 on the
  10-component map. With six new leaf-like modules (schema, scoring, components,
  extract, transcript, unassisted), the density fell and layering carries less. Whether
  R_layer adds anything over R_base is a property of the tree at the time, so it needs
  recomputing per repository, not quoting.

## What we rejected

- Raising the 30-component cap so that telltale's 72 modules count. Trumpower, Sharara and
  Goldsmith validated 5 to 30 concepts (design 2.2). Past that the owner rates thousands
  of pairs, and the cap exists for that reason.
- Counting partial items toward supply. Their truth carries a known blind spot, and V2
  (preregistration 1.7) excludes such items from the primary. The rule was fixed on
  full-coverage items before the run.

## How sure we are

- The counts are deterministic: X1 at a named commit, no sampling. A rerun at the same
  commits gives the same numbers. The script was rerun after a refactor and its output
  was byte-identical.
- The layer-rule Brier is in-sample: the rule sees the graph it is scored on. It bounds
  what layering can know and is not R_layer's out-of-sample score.
- The working-tree figure is a moving target: four builders were editing the tree when
  it was taken.

## What downstream may now say

- "At HEAD e9b5e3a, praxis's mechanical map has 14 product components and 156
  full-coverage D1 items, enough for 39 tasks at 4 items per task. telltale and systemap
  supply none until an owner map is frozen."
- "Every partial component in praxis's working tree is partial only through
  `TYPE_CHECKING` imports."
- Not yet: any supply figure for an owner map, for D2 or for X2 relations, and any claim
  that R_layer is a stronger reference than R_base on these repositories.

Two changes would close the gap. One is an owner map for telltale and for systemap: about
15 components each gives about 210 ordered pairs each. The other is a design decision on
whether a `TYPE_CHECKING` import resolved by X1 is a blind spot or a known edge with
`module_level` false. Treating it as a known edge returns 96 pairs to praxis's
full-coverage pool. That decision is design 3.1's, not this experiment's. Design 3.1
made it on 2026-10-19: a resolved `TYPE_CHECKING` import is a known edge, not a blind
spot. The extractor version for this rule is `x1-v2`.

## Addendum: the flat-package rule (2026-10-19)

### Why this matters (30 seconds)

The run above left telltale and systemap with no structural item until an owner map
exists, because depth 1 gives more than 30 components and depth 0 gives one. The first
fix read the owner's systemap model, which made praxis depend on another project's file
format, and it was withdrawn. The replacement is a mechanical rule for flat packages
(design 2.2 step 5) and `praxis make components`, which prints the mechanical map in
the owner's toml format so that the owner edits a draft instead of writing a map.

### Decision rule, written before the run (2026-10-19)

At each repository's committed HEAD, read from a `git clone --local` under a temporary
`$PRAXIS_HOME` deleted at exit, with `components.derive` as praxis ships it:

- PASS iff (a) telltale and systemap each land in 5 to 34 module groups (a module group
  is a product component holding at least one Python file; 34 is what the measurement
  behind the rule gave systemap), and (b) systemap's module-weighted purity against its
  hand map is at least 0.75.
- Purity labels each Python file directly under the product root with the hand
  component whose `implemented_by` names it, in the repository's map/model.py, read with
  `ast` by the experiment only and never by praxis. A group's purity is its most common
  label's share of its labelled files. Module-weighted purity is the sum of those top
  counts over the sum of labelled files.

Prediction written before the run: telltale about 17 module groups, systemap about 34,
systemap purity about 0.78, praxis unchanged at depth 1.

### What we did

`experiments/E03/flat_groups.py` on praxis (`/home/user/praxis`), telltale
(`/home/user/telltale`) and systemap (`/home/user/0xfauzi/systemap`). The rule runs over
the children of the depth-1 cut. It groups them by the name before the first
underscore, `.py` removed (`page.py` and `page_x.py` both go to `page`). Then, in rounds
until a round moves nothing, each one-file group joins the multi-file group it shares
the most X1 import edges with, in plus out, when that maximum is unique. Every move in a
round is decided on the groups the round started with, so the order of the groups does
not matter.

### What we measured

At HEAD (praxis b208014, telltale ec6a9e4, systemap 02a496b):

| | praxis | telltale | systemap |
|---|---|---|---|
| children at depth 1 | 18 | 72 | 163 |
| prefix groups (multi-file / one-file) | not grouped | 31 (17 / 14) | 74 (23 / 51) |
| product components after attachment | 18, depth 1, no warning | 21 | 40, `above_30_after_grouping` |
| module groups | 18 | 21 | 38 |
| one-file groups left | n/a | 4 | 15 |
| largest groups | n/a | forecast 14, cli 13, experiments 10, providers 8 | page 22, typescript 21, delta 19, model 13, schematic 10 |
| purity, mean per group / module-weighted | 1.0 / 1.0 (one file each) | no hand map | 0.891 / 0.801 against 16 hand components |

(a) FAIL: systemap has 38 module groups, above 34; telltale's 21 is inside. (b) PASS:
0.801. **Rule: FAIL.** The prediction held for praxis and for purity, and missed for the
counts by 4 on each flat repository.

### What it means

- telltale now has a mechanical map with 21 components, inside 5 to 30, so it can be
  frozen and supply D1 items without an owner map.
- systemap's 40 components are refused by `praxis set components`, which prints the
  draft. The owner merges at least 10 groups, or runs `praxis make components
  --write` and edits the file. The groups the owner starts from agree with the owner's
  own map on 80 percent of files.
- The gap to the measurement behind the rule has two sources. One is ties: every one of
  systemap's 15 one-file Python groups left, and telltale's `daemon_capture`, `env`,
  `receiver` and `stats`, has two or three groups tied for most edges under X1, so the
  rule leaves it alone. systemap ends with 4 more one-file groups than that measurement
  and telltale with 2. The other is children that measurement left out: telltale's
  `forecast/` and `providers/` directories, and systemap's `py.typed` and `fonts/`,
  which hold no Python. The rule counts every child, because a path no rule maps is
  `unmapped`. The non-empty `__init__.py` (`.`) joined a group in both repositories.

### What we rejected

- Breaking ties by the order of the groups. Run in sorted order, one group at a time,
  the rounds give systemap 36 module groups and telltale 19, but the result then depends
  on the names of the groups, not the structure. This was run once as a descriptive
  check and is not the rule.
- Weighting by import statements instead of distinct module edges. That gives systemap
  34 module groups and telltale 21. It was run once as a descriptive check. Adopting it
  after seeing the rule fail would be choosing the instrument by its result.
- Raising the 30 cap (as in the run above).

### How sure we are

- The counts are deterministic: X1 at a named commit, no sampling, and the attachment is
  independent of group order by construction.
- Purity is against one owner's hand map of one repository; it says the groups are
  recognisable, not that they are the owner's concepts.

### What downstream may now say

- "At ec6a9e4, telltale's mechanical map has 21 components under the flat-package rule."
- "At 02a496b, systemap's has 40, so it needs an owner merge before freezing; the draft
  agrees with the owner's hand map on 0.80 of files."
- Not yet: any supply figure for telltale's 21-component map (E03's pool count has not
  been rerun on it), and any claim that the grouped components are the owner's concepts.
